# E056 selected as the STS baseline — November 8, 2026

**Status:** selected configuration for extension to new sit-to-stand trials, not yet independently biomechanically validated. The earlier 72-run convergence closeout remains historical and unchanged.

## What was screened

- 72/72 E015–E086 result archives successfully checked against a common measured-force reference; 50/72 IPOPT-optimal.
- Force/COP screening used a fixed **0.47–1.47 s, 53-point grid**. This is the **ranking window**, not the full E056 simulation time span (approximately **0.17–1.81 s**).
- Provisional ranking included only converged results with sufficient valid COP samples. It used a heuristic combination of relative GRF, COP and vertical free-moment errors (60%/30%/10%). These weights are *not* validated clinical or biomechanical acceptance criteria.
- E056, E032 and E058 were inspected against a MoCap-derived IK motion in a 3D visualizer. The observer reported all three as looking close to reference; E058 showed a small abrupt movement at the final frame. This was a *qualitative* check, not joint-angle RMSE.

## Selected E056

- **Profile:** STS-inspired kinematic tracking (position/velocity/acceleration weights 50/10/50), not identical to the original OpenSimAD STS implementation and not simply Emily's full parameter set.
- **Other tracking weights:** GRF 0.001; COP 300; COP monotonic 0.001; pelvis residuals 0.001; coordinate excitation 10. Additional effective weights are in `../configs/E056_selected_config.json`.
- **Solver:** torque-driven, 100 mesh density, IPOPT tolerance `0.01` (runner-specific `ipopt_tolerance=2`), 10,000 maximum iterations, pelvis residuals allowed, periodic constraints disabled, heel minimum vertical GRF 0 N.
- **Input trial:** `subject2_lab_STS_test / STS1`, buffered window 0.17–1.81 s.
- **Tracking errors:** bilateral 3D GRF RMSE **10.01 N**, bilateral COP 2D RMSE **19.18 mm**, vertical free-moment RMSE **2.51 N·m** over 0.47–1.47 s.

These are prediction-vs-target errors on the *same data used to optimize tracking*; they are not out-of-sample model validation. The short-window aggregate metric is not a guarantee of valid contact dynamics at the beginning or end of the buffered motion.

## Controlled comparisons and tolerance sensitivity

| Comparison | Result |
|---|---|
| E056 (COP ON) vs E058 (COP OFF) | Both optimal; GRF RMSE 10.01 vs 9.50 N, COP RMSE 19.18 vs 32.63 mm. The enabled switch affects both COP tracking and monotonic terms; it does not isolate either term individually. |
| E056 vs E032 | Both COP ON, buffered, mesh 100, tolerance 0.01; STS-inspired vs Emily kinematic tracking weights. Both looked similar to reference visually. |
| E056 vs E055 | Same profile/mesh/tolerance/COP setting; buffered vs original time window. |
| E017 (tol 0.01) vs E021 (tol 0.001) | Same 317 iterations, identical exported motion SHA-256, and final scaled NLP error approximately 6.8583e-5. |
| E056 (tol 0.01) vs E060 (tol 0.001) | E056 optimal in 541 iterations, final scaled NLP error approximately 0.00829; E060 continued to 8,144 iterations and ended nonoptimally. Available plots of `inf_pr` and `inf_du` overlay through iteration 541. Confirm the exact E060 termination message from archived logs before explaining its failure mechanism. |

Across the full sweep, COP OFF achieved 36/36 IPOPT-optimal outcomes; COP ON achieved 14/36. At tolerance 0.001, COP ON achieved 0/18; at 0.01, COP ON achieved 14/18. This strong interaction is descriptive of *this trial and implementation*, not an expected universal outcome.

## Unresolved before muscle static optimization

2. Verify input and predicted force frames, signs, units, foot assignment, time alignment and COP moment conventions.
3. Quantify stance-foot drift and contact geometry/penetration.

5. Inspect the free-moment/COP systematic offsets and their impact on knee and hip moment arms.

## Provenance and preservation

The **actual** E056 manifest and `ExperimentMetadata/E056.json` were read from the CHTC archive and used to build `../configs/E056_selected_config.json`. Preserve the original CHTC archive separately under institutional research data retention policy. This public GitHub branch intentionally excludes participant recordings, markers, motion trajectories, subject-specific model/force archives, and credentials.

For a new STS trial, use [the adaptation checklist](apply_E056_to_new_STS.md); do not reuse the old trial data or claim the baseline's validation metrics generalize to a new subject.
